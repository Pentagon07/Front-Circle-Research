Who We Are

About Front Circle Research

We exist to make institutional-calibre research accessible, precise, and independent — free from the conflicts that cloud conventional coverage.

Our Mission

Research with conviction, delivered without compromise

Front Circle Research was founded on the belief that rigorous, conflict-free research should not be the exclusive domain of bulge-bracket institutions. We combine systematic quantitative frameworks with deep fundamental analysis to illuminate market opportunities that conventional coverage overlooks.

Every report is independently reviewed before publication. We hold no positions in any securities we cover, and we receive no compensation from issuers or third parties for our research conclusions.

Our Principles

What guides every report

◉
Independence

No issuer payments, no advisory relationships, no index inclusion pressures. Our conclusions are driven exclusively by evidence.

◆
Rigour

Every report is built from primary source data, peer-reviewed frameworks, and multi-scenario stress testing before publication.

◈
Transparency

Assumptions are stated explicitly. Uncertainty is quantified, not buried. We show our work and invite scrutiny.

Process

How we produce research

Step 01
Idea Generation

Systematic screening of public filings, macro data releases, and market dislocations to identify coverage candidates.

Step 02
Fundamental Analysis

Deep-dive into financial statements, competitive dynamics, management quality, and industry structure.

Step 03
Quantitative Modelling

Construction of multi-year financial models, DCF valuations, scenario matrices, and sensitivity analyses.

Step 04
Peer Review

All research is reviewed by a second analyst before publication. Methodology challenges are mandatory.

Step 05
Publication

Reports are published with explicit ratings, price targets, and full assumption disclosure. Post-publication tracking is maintained.

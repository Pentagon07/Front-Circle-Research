Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

Front Circle Research collects information you provide directly, including your name, email address, organisation, and the reason for requesting research access. We do not collect any financial or payment information.

We may also collect limited technical data (such as browser type and approximate geographic region) to maintain site security and improve the user experience. We do not deploy tracking cookies or advertising pixels.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information provided through our report request or contact forms is used solely to respond to your enquiry, assess your research access request, and — where you have opted in — to send you publication notifications.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for commercial purposes. Information may be shared with service providers who assist in our operations (such as email delivery), under strict confidentiality agreements.

3. Data Retention

Contact and request information is retained for up to 24 months, after which it is securely deleted unless a longer retention period is required by applicable law.

4. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data from unauthorised access or disclosure. Our site does not accept or store payment data.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of any personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, please contact us at frontcircleresearch@gmail.com.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. Material changes will be communicated via a notice on our homepage.

7. Contact

Questions regarding this policy should be directed to: frontcircleresearch@gmail.com
